# Miniature Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Miniature** (the “App”), provided by **[Developer / SANGHOON HAN]** (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad. Please read it carefully. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here.

> **In short**
>
> - The App has no accounts, and we do not run a server that receives your photos, videos or personal information.
> - Your photos and videos are processed **only on your device** and are never uploaded by us.
> - The App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**, which may collect certain device and usage information.
> - Payments for the optional in-app purchase are handled entirely by **Apple**.

## 1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or payment card details. We do not operate any backend service that stores information about you. We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Photos, videos and camera

The App lets you choose a photo or video from your device, or take one with your camera, and apply a tilt-shift (miniature) effect to it.

- **Processing happens on your device.** The images and videos you select are processed locally and are not transmitted to us or to any server operated by us.
- **Camera.** If you choose to take a new photo, the App asks for camera access through iOS. The captured photo is used only for editing inside the App.
- **Saving and exporting.** When you export a result, the App uses the iOS share sheet and/or asks for permission to *add* photos to your Photos library. The App requests add-only access; it does not read your existing Photos library through that permission. Where you pick a photo or video, you choose the specific item to share with the App.

You can change these permissions at any time in *Settings › Privacy & Security* on your device.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services, Google and its partners may collect and process information from your device, which may include:

- Device identifiers (such as an advertising identifier where available and permitted, or other device or app identifiers);
- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- Device and software information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type);
- Ad interaction and usage data (ads shown or tapped, session information);
- Diagnostics and performance data (such as crash and error information).

This information is collected by Google directly through its SDK; we do not receive your personal information from it, although we may see aggregated reports about ad performance and revenue. How Google uses information collected from apps that use its services is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and Google’s general privacy policy is at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your advertising choices

- **iOS tracking permission.** On iOS you can control whether apps may request to track you across other companies’ apps and websites in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- **Personalized ads.** You can manage Google ad personalization at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
- **Apple advertising.** You can manage Apple’s personalized advertising in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising*.
- **Remove ads.** Purchasing the optional premium unlock (see below) removes ads from the App.

Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), ads are shown in accordance with the consent choices that you make through the consent message provided in the App.

## 4. In-app purchase (Apple)

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (“Premium”) that unlocks additional features and removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We do **not** receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account information.

To unlock Premium on your device, the App uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to check, on your device, whether your Apple Account has purchased Premium. This check uses transaction information provided and verified by Apple. We do not transfer this information to our own servers. Apple’s handling of your information is described in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can restore a previous purchase at any time using the “Restore Purchase” button in the App.

## 5. Third-party content libraries

To render its interface, the App loads an open-source JavaScript library (jQuery) from a public content delivery network (cdnjs, operated by Cloudflare, Inc.). When this happens, the delivery network may receive technical information that is inherent to any web request, such as your IP address and device/browser type. No photos, videos or personal information from the App are included in this request.

## 6. Data retention and security

Because we do not collect your photos, videos or personal information, we do not store them. Files you export are saved on your device or shared by you using the destinations you choose; they are then under your control. Data collected by Google AdMob and Apple is retained according to their own policies. We rely on the security protections of iOS and of the third-party services described above, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the minimum age required by the laws of your country, such as 14 in the Republic of Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve the matter.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of it, or to withdraw consent. This may include rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you ourselves, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete. For data held by Google or Apple, please use the controls provided by those companies (see Sections 3 and 4) or contact them directly. You can also contact us with any request or question, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Information collected by Google may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, subject to Google’s safeguards and applicable law.
